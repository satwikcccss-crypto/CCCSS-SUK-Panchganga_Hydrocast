HYDROLOGIC SOLVER SPECS

SCS-CN RUNOFF PARTITION STANDARDIZED
Pe = (P − Ia)² / (P − Ia + S)
Governing runoff depth equation across all 9 Panchganga subbasins. Ia is the initial abstraction (canopy interception + depression storage), calibrated at λ = 0.20 (standard) down to 0.08 in saturated monsoon peaks.
SCS DIMENSIONLESS UNIT HYDROGRAPH USACE METHOD
u(t) = (t/tp)³·⁷ · exp[3.7 · (1 − t/tp)]
Transforms excess rainfall Pe(t) into direct surface runoff hydrograph. Time to peak tp = Δt/2 + lag/60. Subbasin lag times range from 35.3h (Padasali) to 86.7h (Radhanagari).
MUSKINGUM CHANNEL ROUTING MASS CONSERVING
C₀ = (Δt − 2KX) / (2K(1−X) + Δt)
Routes flow pulses down the 5 river channels. Sub-stepping is enforced so that 2KX ≤ Δt ≤ 2K(1-X), strictly preventing numerical diffusion and artificial oscillations.
